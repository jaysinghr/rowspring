Rowspring

Turn spreadsheet rows into Google Calendar events

A Google Sheets add-on. The sheet is the source of truth.

Rowspring keeps a Google Calendar of your choice in sync with an Events sheet. New rows create events, edits update them, and setting a row's status to DELETE removes the event.

How it works

  • Set up the Events and Log sheets with one click.
  • Click Sync calendar, or turn on automatic sync every 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or hour.
  • Every change is recorded in the Log sheet.

Your data stays in your Google account

Rowspring runs entirely inside your Google account. It has no servers and the developer never receives your spreadsheet or calendar data. See the Privacy Policy.
