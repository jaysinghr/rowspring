Rowspring

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Rowspring ("Rowspring", "we") is a Google Sheets add-on that syncs rows in a spreadsheet to events on a Google Calendar. This policy explains what data it accesses and how it is handled.

Data Rowspring accesses

  • The spreadsheet where Rowspring is installed. It reads the Events sheet (titles, start and end times, all-day flags, status, and event IDs) and writes event IDs back and appends entries to a Log sheet. Rowspring is limited to the current spreadsheet (spreadsheets.currentonly scope).
  • Your Google Calendar. It lists the calendars you own so you can choose one, and creates, updates, and deletes events on the calendar you select (calendar.calendarlist.readonly and calendar.events scopes). It only changes events that rows in your sheet are linked to, or creates new ones for new rows.
  • Add-on settings. Your calendar choice, sync schedule, and last-run status are stored in the spreadsheet's document properties inside your Google account.

How data is used and shared

This data is used only to provide the sync you request. Rowspring runs inside Google Apps Script in your own Google account. We operate no servers or databases, and the developer does not receive, store, or have access to your spreadsheet or calendar data. We do not sell data, share it with third parties, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models, and no human reads it.

Rowspring's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Logs

Sync activity is written to the Log sheet in your spreadsheet. Error details may also appear in Google's Apps Script execution logs for your account, which Google manages under its own policies.

Retention and deletion

Rowspring keeps no data of its own. Your data stays in your spreadsheet and calendar until you delete it. To stop Rowspring, turn automatic sync off in Settings, then remove the add-on from Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Events already created remain on your calendar unless you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
